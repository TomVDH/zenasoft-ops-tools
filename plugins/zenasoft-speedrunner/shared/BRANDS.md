is current as of the day your project was set up.

- ESM Software
- PacePlus
- Provitrac

- ZenaDrone
- ZenaStudios
- Zoo Office

If a marketer asks for one of these, or for a brand not listed anywhere here, say
exactly this and stop:

> The brand will be added on a next update. If urgent, please ask your Ops Person
> about it.

Do not build anything, do not borrow another brand's colours, and do not guess a
date. See "A brand you cannot build for" in the instructions.

## If a brand is on neither list

Some ZenaTech properties are not marketing brands, and some are too new to have a
brand guide. Treat an unlisted name exactly like a pending one: say the line above.
Never assume a name is a typo for a brand that IS available.
